## Description

HabitWise helps you pause, understand what may be underneath a food craving, and choose a response that fits.

Use a guided check-in to explore body needs, emotions, environmental cues, familiar habits, and sensory preferences. Get a detailed plan with realistic steps and options. Keep a private history and discover descriptive patterns after enough check-ins.

HabitWise puts hunger first. If you are hungry, the plan is to eat. There are no calorie counts, weight goals, moral food labels, willpower scores, or streaks to protect.

Optional health and medication context can filter unsuitable tools and account for patterns you report, such as appetite returning as an ADHD medication wears off. HabitWise does not diagnose conditions or recommend medication changes.

Your data stays in an encrypted database on your device. No account is required. Export or delete your data whenever you choose.

HabitWise is a self-reflection and behavior-support tool, not emergency care or a substitute for professional medical advice.
