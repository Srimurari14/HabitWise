# Google Play metadata draft

## App name

HabitWise

## Short description

Private, judgment-free craving check-ins and practical support plans.

## Full description

Understand the urge and choose what helps.

HabitWise guides you through a quick, private check-in for hunger, emotions, environmental cues, habit loops, and sensory needs. It then offers a detailed, practical plan with steps and accessible options.

Your history and insights stay on your device. After enough entries, HabitWise shows careful, descriptive patterns with sample sizes—never a diagnosis or a claim that one factor caused a craving.

Hunger comes first. HabitWise does not count calories, set weight targets, shame food choices, or score willpower. Eating the food you want is always an allowed outcome.

Optional health and medication context can remove unsuitable tools and account for patterns you personally report, including appetite returning as an ADHD medication wears off. The app does not recommend medication changes or replace professional care.

Features:
- Offline-first encrypted storage
- No account or advertising
- Guided craving plans
- Neutral history and insights
- Optional device-local reminders
- JSON export and complete local deletion
- Light and dark themes

HabitWise is a self-reflection and behavior-support tool. It is not emergency care, a diagnostic tool, a glucose-treatment calculator, or a substitute for a clinician or dietitian.
